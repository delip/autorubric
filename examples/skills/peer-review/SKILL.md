# Scientific Peer Review

## When to Use
Use when asked to review, critique, or evaluate a scientific research paper or manuscript.

## Review Procedure

### Step 1: Summarize the Paper
Write a 2-3 sentence summary of the paper's main contribution, methodology, and key findings.

### Step 2: Assess Methodology
- Identify the study design (RCT, observational, meta-analysis, etc.)
- Evaluate whether methodology matches the research question
- Check for controls, blinding, randomization as applicable
- Note methodological limitations

### Step 3: Evaluate Statistical Analysis
- Check whether tests are appropriate for the data type
- Look for multiple comparisons corrections if applicable
- Evaluate sample size adequacy
- Assess effect sizes, not just significance

### Step 4: Identify Strengths
List 2-4 specific strengths with concrete references to the paper.

### Step 5: Identify Weaknesses
List 2-4 specific weaknesses with constructive suggestions for improvement.

### Step 6: Questions for Authors
List 1-3 questions that would clarify aspects of the work.

### Step 7: Recommendation
Provide one of: Accept, Minor Revision, Major Revision, Reject. Justify in 1-2 sentences.

## Formatting
- Use section headers for each step
- Reference specific sections, figures, or results
- Be constructive — suggest improvements, not just problems
- Keep under 800 words
